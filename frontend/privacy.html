Your information.

A plain-language description of the current Nexora service. Updated 24 October 2026.

About this notice

Nexora is preparing for public launch. This page describes the current application’s data handling. The operator’s legal identity, privacy contact and retention schedule still need to be published before public bookings open.

Business details will be published before public bookings open.

A public privacy/support contact has not been published yet.

Information the service handles

  • Accounts: names, email addresses, phone numbers, password hashes and session records. Recovery codes are stored as hashes.
  • Journeys: pickup and destination addresses and coordinates, route details, fares, status updates, ratings and payment records.
  • Deliveries: package details, sender and recipient contact information, handoff PINs, recipient name, signature and delivery timestamps.
  • Drivers: vehicle and onboarding details, availability, earnings and location updates while using the driver service.
  • Optional notifications: a browser push subscription if you enable alerts.

How information is used and shared

These details support account access, matching and dispatch, route estimates, journey tracking, handoff verification and payment records. Assigned drivers receive the job information they need. Authorized operations staff can access records for service administration.

Configured providers process data for hosting, database storage, address search and routing, notifications and card payments. The map uses OpenStreetMap tiles and address search uses Mapbox when enabled. Loading maps or externally hosted fonts and libraries sends ordinary network request information, including your IP address, to those providers. When card checkout is enabled, Stripe handles card entry and payment processing.

A delivery tracking link provides access to delivery progress to anyone who has the link. Share it only with people who need to follow the delivery.

On your device

Nexora uses browser session storage for sign-in tokens and pending booking requests. Homepage route drafts stay in the current tab’s session storage for up to 30 minutes and are removed when carried into the matching booking page. Addresses are not placed in navigation URLs. Browser storage may remain until the tab is closed or you sign out; signing out clears the account session.

Location access is requested through your browser when you choose to use it. You can refuse or revoke access in browser settings. You can also disable notifications there.

Access, correction and deletion

You can view your journey records when signed in and sign out on a shared device. Account deletion and personal-data export are not currently self-service features. A contact channel and the applicable retention and request-handling details must be published before public launch.
