Planning and confirming a journey

Enter accurate addresses and contact details. A route or fare preview is not a confirmed booking or a guarantee of driver availability. Review the displayed price and payment method before confirming. Quotes expire; request a new fare if your quote is no longer valid or your details change.

Payments and cancellations

Cash rides are paid to the driver at the end of the trip. For deliveries, the recipient pays the fee in cash on arrival. The sender should confirm this arrangement with the recipient before booking. Card payments are offered only when enabled in checkout.

Use the cancellation control on an eligible booking. Availability depends on the journey’s stage. The app does not currently provide a self-service dispute or refund workflow; check the help page for contact availability.

Packages and handoffs

Describe your package and weight accurately, provide contact details for the people involved, and have the package ready for collection. Do not use the service for unlawful goods or activities. Mark items that need careful handling. Scheduled pickup and insurance are unavailable.

Pickup and delivery PINs verify handoffs. The recipient provides a signature at delivery. Only share each PIN when its handoff is taking place.

Accounts and drivers

Keep your credentials and recovery code private. Drivers must submit their account and vehicle details for approval before accepting jobs. Administrative approval is not a representation of a particular background-check or insurance program.

Read the privacy information and safety guidance. Use local emergency services for an emergency.
