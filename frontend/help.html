BEFORE YOU GO

A few checks for every journey.

  • Check the driver’s name and vehicle plate in your ride details before getting in.
  • Share your pickup PIN only after you have checked the driver and vehicle.
  • Keep delivery PINs private until the relevant package handoff. A tracking link can be viewed by anyone you share it with.
  • If you are in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services. Nexora’s website is not an emergency response service.

Rides & payments

How do I book a ride?

Choose Book a ride, enter your pickup and destination, and sign in when prompted. Search for each address and select a result, or choose a point on the map. Check the fare, review the payment method, then confirm. The app will show driver updates when someone is assigned.

Why can’t I request a booking?

New bookings are paused while launch setup is completed. Existing customers can sign in to view their records. The availability banner on booking pages reflects the current service configuration.

When do I pay?

For a cash ride, pay the driver at the end of the journey. Card checkout appears only when enabled. For cards, an authorization is made before booking and the charge is captured after the ride; an unused hold is released. Your bank controls when a released hold disappears.

Can I change or cancel?

Before confirming, choose Change journey or Edit details to get a new quote. For an existing request, use the Cancel action when it is available in your ride or delivery details. Cancellation is unavailable after certain journey stages.

Sending a package

What do I need to send a package?

Have the package ready for collection. Enter pickup and delivery addresses, sender and recipient contact details, a description, and the weight. Confirm with the recipient that they can pay the delivery fee in cash. Review your fare before confirming. Scheduled pickup and insurance are unavailable.

How do I track and confirm delivery?

Open Your deliveries for the tracking link and handoff PINs. Give the pickup PIN to the driver when the package is collected. The recipient gives the delivery PIN and signs when it arrives. Completed delivery details show the recipient and payment receipt when recorded.

Your account

I forgot my password.

Use the recovery code you previously saved on the account recovery page. You can create a code while you still know your password. Without the password or a saved code, self-service recovery is unavailable. Email recovery is not enabled.

How is my information used?

Account, route and contact details are used to operate your journeys. Read the privacy information for the data currently handled by Nexora.
